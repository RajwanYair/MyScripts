# Yom Kippur Synagogue Prayer Times Sign — Image Creation Specification

## Goal

Create a dignified, premium, highly legible **16:9 horizontal landscape digital signage image** for a synagogue TV, LED screen, projector, or large display.

The design should feel sacred, traditional, calm, welcoming, elegant, and photorealistic. The composition must feel **flowing and organic rather than square, rigid, or overly boxed-in**.

The most important requirement is **exact Hebrew text fidelity and readability**. Do not paraphrase, translate, correct, omit, reorder, or invent any Hebrew text. Preserve punctuation, numbers, times, spacing, and wording exactly as supplied below.

---

## Overall Composition

- Format: 16:9 landscape.
- Hebrew composition should follow a natural right-to-left reading direction.
- Use a wide central warm ivory/cream marble panel with subtle realistic gold veining.
- Add a narrow, elegant dark-wood luxury frame around the outer edge.
- The central design should occupy approximately 90% of the canvas.
- Avoid a rigid poster-grid appearance.
- Use curved, flowing decorative transitions and generous breathing room.
- Do not make the image look square or crowded.
- Avoid excessive ornamentation and unnecessary boxes.
- The prayer schedules may use two clean tables because this greatly improves readability, but the tables should have elegant rounded or softly framed edges and integrate naturally into the flowing composition.

---

## Color Palette

Use a coherent premium synagogue palette:

- Warm ivory / cream marble
- Dark walnut or mahogany wood
- Deep navy blue for primary text
- Deep olive green for headings and secondary emphasis
- Muted antique gold for accents, borders, separators, and decorative details

Suggested colors:

- Navy: `#1D2A44`
- Antique gold: `#D4AF37`
- Deep olive green: approximately `#2F4F2F`

Use strong contrast between text and background.

---

## Typography

Use a dignified, traditional, highly legible Hebrew typeface.

### Requirements

- Hebrew must be rendered accurately.
- Do not replace Hebrew letters with visually similar characters.
- Do not invent or alter words.
- Preserve all punctuation exactly.
- Preserve all numbers and times exactly.
- Use large text suitable for viewing from the back of a prayer hall.
- The title should be centered at the top.
- Headings should be visually distinct from body text.
- Prayer names and times must be extremely easy to associate with one another.
- In the two schedule sections, use a clear table with:
  - one column for `סוג התפילה`
  - one column for `השעה`
- Keep each prayer and its time on the same row.
- Use sufficient row spacing and horizontal alignment.
- Do not let decorative objects overlap any Hebrew text.

## Decorative Visual Elements

Decorative imagery should support the Yom Kippur theme without competing with the prayer schedule.

## Upper Right — Bright Sky

Replace the previous fruit decoration completely.

Create a beautiful, photorealistic scene in the upper-right corner:

- Bright, clear blue sky
- Soft luminous white clouds
- Warm heavenly light
- A refined golden sunbeam emerging through the clouds
- The scene should feel spiritual and uplifting, not dramatic or fantasy-like
- Do not cover the main title or schedule tables

There should be **no grapes, pomegranates, dates, figs, wheat, or Seven Species arrangement in the upper-right corner**.

---

## Left Side — Synagogue Entrance

Include a photorealistic Jerusalem-style synagogue entrance along the left margin.

The entrance should have a stone architectural appearance and a tasteful sign reading exactly:

אהל דוד

Keep the entrance partially integrated into the background so that it frames the composition without making the poster feel divided into rigid columns.

---

## Lower Left — Yom Kippur Prayer Items

Because tefillin are not worn on Yom Kippur, **do not show tefillin anywhere in the design**.

Instead, create a tasteful photorealistic arrangement:

- A white tallit spread in a graceful, stepped/layered arrangement across a wooden table
- The tallit should be clearly white and elegant
- A clean, white, button-down shirt lying unfolded beneath the machzor
- A Yom Kippur machzor placed naturally on top of the white shirt
- The machzor should look like a traditional Jewish High Holy Days prayer book
- No tefillin
- No tefillin boxes
- No black tefillin straps

The arrangement should feel natural and photographic, not like a product catalog.

---

## Lower Right — Time / Prayer Symbolism

Include a refined antique brass clock near the lower-right area.

The clock should:

- Be photorealistic
- Have a tasteful antique gold/brass finish
- Reinforce the idea of prayer at the proper time
- Remain visually secondary to the schedule
- Not obscure any table row

Subtle olive branches or restrained botanical details may frame the clock, but they must not become a fruit display.

---

## Bottom Decorative Blessing

At the bottom of the image, use a flowing navy-and-gold curved decorative banner integrated into the wooden frame.

The Hebrew text must be reproduced **exactly**:

גמר חתימה טובה

This wording is critical.

Do not omit any letter.

Do not replace the phrase with another blessing.

Do not add any additional words.

---

## Layout and Readability Priority

The schedule is the most important functional part of the image after the title.

The two schedule sections should therefore be presented as **clear, highly readable tables** rather than loose paragraphs.

## Recommended visual hierarchy

1. Large centered title:
   `מרכז רוחני אהל דוד`

2. Subtitle:
   `הודעות וועד בית הכנסת`

3. Main heading:
   `זמני תפילות יום הכיפורים ה'תשפ"ז:`

4. Introductory line:
   `ערב יום הכיפורים, סליחות, התרת נדרים ותפילה בשעות: 23:50, 05:00, 06:00, 07:00, 08:00, 09:00`

5. Single schedule tables:
   - `זמני תפילות יום הכיפורים:`

6. Bottom blessing:
   `גמר חתימה טובה`

---

## Table Design

The tables should be elegant and readable rather than heavy.

Recommended treatment:

- Cream or ivory table background
- Thin antique-gold grid lines
- Deep olive or navy table headers
- Large dark-navy Hebrew text
- Large, clearly aligned times
- Consistent row heights
- Strong visual separation between prayer type and time
- No unnecessary icons inside the table
- No photographic objects behind the tables
- No decorative elements touching the text

The time column should be visually aligned so that all times can be scanned vertically.

---

## Flowing, Non-Square Composition

A major design requirement is that the image must **not look like a rigid square poster**.

Use:

- Gentle curves
- Organic framing
- A flowing wood border
- Softly rounded or subtly curved table containers
- Natural overlap between architectural and photographic elements
- Open marble space around the central typography
- Decorative elements concentrated near the margins
- A curved bottom banner

Avoid:

- Multiple independent rectangular boxes
- Heavy grid structures
- A boxed-in central panel
- Excessive vertical columns
- Dense decorations
- Symmetrical clutter
- Objects competing with the Hebrew text

The final result should feel like a premium synagogue digital sign that naturally flows across a wide screen.

---

## Photorealism

All decorative objects should have photographic quality:

- Realistic lighting
- Natural shadows
- Realistic marble texture
- Realistic wood grain
- Natural fabric folds
- Authentic-looking tallit
- Authentic-looking machzor
- Realistic stone architecture
- Realistic brass clock
- Natural clouds and sunlight

Avoid cartoon graphics, clip-art, flat illustrations, overly glossy 3D objects, or fantasy effects.
